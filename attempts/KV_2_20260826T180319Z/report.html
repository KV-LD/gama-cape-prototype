Capstone problem

1. Business Context  
Medora Community Health is a mid-size multi-clinic network facing rising no-show rates and fragmented intake across primary care, behavioral health, and specialty referrals. Leadership wants a discovery-backed product brief for an AI-assisted care-navigation experience that clinicians and front-desk staff will actually adopt, with Claude used only as a drafting and synthesis aid during discovery—not as a production runtime dependency. The FDE trainee is embedded for two weeks to turn raw stakeholder input into a decision-ready scope package the COO and CMIO can fund or kill.

2. Explicit Constraints  
- Discovery window is ten business days; no production data leaves the clinic firewall and only de-identified operational metrics or synthetic transcripts may be used.  
- Claude may be used for interview synthesis, requirement clustering, and prioritization matrix drafting; all outputs must be human-reviewed and cite source stakeholder statements.  
- No hyperscaler services or cloud deployment assumptions are in scope—deliverables remain vendor- and platform-agnostic documents.  
- Final package must fit a 12-page limit (appendices excluded) suitable for an exec steering committee read-out.

3. Deliverable Spec  
Submit a single Discovery & Framing Brief containing:  
- Stakeholder Discovery evidence: structured discovery notes or interview records that identify at least five stakeholder roles (e.g., front-desk lead, care coordinator, CMIO, patient advocate, revenue-cycle manager), state explicit hypotheses tested with each, and show question traces that go beyond feature wish-lists to goals, constraints, and failure modes.  
- Requirement Translation evidence: a business problem statement followed by clearly separated functional and non-functional requirements, each requirement tagged with the originating stakeholder quote or observation and traced back to the problem statement.  
- Use-Case Prioritization evidence: a value-vs-complexity or ROI-scored prioritization artefact (table or matrix) covering the candidate use cases surfaced in discovery, with the scoring method, weights, and raw scores visible so the ranking is reproducible rather than gut-feel.  
Every section must be usable as direct evidence against the three skill indices above.

4. One Deliberate Complication  
Midway through the engagement the CMIO freezes any use case that touches behavioral-health notes, citing an upcoming external audit, while the COO simultaneously demands that behavioral-health no-show reduction remain the headline ROI metric in the prioritization—forcing an explicit re-scope and re-score without expanding the page limit.
